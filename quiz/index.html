Suggested 60-minute flow

  • Icebreaker — 10 min (Tab 2)
  • Rapid-Fire — 15 min (Tab 3)
  • Need or Desire? — 15 min (Tab 8)
  • Bingo — 15 min (Tab 10)
  • Final scores & prizes — 5 min

Suggested 90-minute flow

The 60-minute flow, plus:

  • Kahoot-style Lightning — 10 min (Tab 13), straight after Rapid-Fire
  • Finish Sri Babuji's Line — 12 min (Tab 4)
  • Analogy Charades — 15 min (Tab 6)

…and run Whose Words? (Tab 7, 10 min) as the warm-up instead of one other round.

Match Pairs (Tab 14) and Flashcards (Tab 15) are the spare parts — use them while teams form, or as a cooldown while the final scores are tallied.

Team setup

3–4 teams, 4–6 people each. Mix veterans and newcomers — the Human Timeline icebreaker (Tab 2) folds the room into balanced teams for you.

Each team picks a name — +20 bonus for the best Sai-flavoured name (facilitator judges).

Scoring defaults

  • Rapid-Fire: +10 / −5
  • Finish the Line: +10
  • True / False: +5
  • Charades: 5 + 5 (analogy + teaching)
  • Whose Words?: +10
  • Need or Desire?: +5 per correct team call
  • Bingo: first full row / column / diagonal wins the round
  • Teach-Back finale: scored by applause

Tie-breaker: sudden-death Finish Sri Babuji's Line — one line per tied team; miss and you're out.

Icebreakers

Warm the room up before a single point is scored. Pick one — or run the Human Timeline last, because it builds your teams.

HOW TO RUN

1 · Find Your Satsang Twin (10 min): hand every person a printed mixer sheet (print button below). Mingle — one signature per square from someone it is true of. First completed row, column or diagonal shouts and wins.

2 · Two Truths and a Leela (5 min per table): each person states two true things about their spiritual journey and one invented "leela that happened to me". The table guesses which one is fiction.

3 · The Human Timeline (5 min): everyone lines up without talking — fingers and charades only — ordered by years since first hearing of Sai Baba. Then fold the line in half to mix veterans and newcomers into balanced quiz teams.

Two Truths and a Leela

Say this: "Tell us two true things about your spiritual journey — and one leela that never actually happened to you. Make the leela sound real. Table, your job: spot the fiction."

Five minutes per table. The best invented leelas usually sound almost too neat — enjoy the detective work.

The Human Timeline

Say this: "No talking. Fingers and acting only. Line up by how many years it has been since you first heard of Sai Baba — longest at this end, newest at that end."

When the line settles, fold it in half: the veteran end pairs with the newcomer end. Count off into 3–4 teams of 4–6 — every team now has both memory and fresh eyes.

Twelve questions, fifteen seconds each. Fast hands, faster memories.

True or False

Eight statements. Some of them sound exactly like things Sri Babuji would say — that's the trap.

HOW TO RUN

Read the statement. On three, every team holds up green (true) or red (false). Tap the card to reveal the verdict and the one-line why. +5 per correct call. Wrong-colour waving is part of the fun — keep the pace brisk.

1

Sri Babuji says udi is a symbol of vairagya and Vedanta.

FALSE — udi is Baba's padadhuli, the dust of His feet; "there is no vairagya in it, no Vedanta."
2

True parayana means finishing a fixed number of Satcharitra pages every day.

FALSE — marking pages ("today 12 pages") is not parayana; true parayana is tattva-enquiry: take ONE leela and ask it 100 questions with real jijnasa, until "we disappear".
3

Baba sometimes withholds a prayer on purpose, because granting it would make us suffer.

TRUE — He knows better than the wish itself.43:27
4

A woman in a ₹5000 Kanchi pattu saree walks happily.

FALSE — the saree is a heavy burden, borne for nothing but one word of praise: "how beautiful".
5

Ramana's "Who am I?" was his brahmastra for silencing pestering questioners.

TRUE.
6

When the lights went out, the disciple was praised for lighting a different candle than asked.

FALSE — he was scolded for days: exact obedience to the guru's word, not creative substitution.46:41
7

Jesus said "whoever has seen me has seen the Father" — and Sri Babuji links it to Baba's reply to Das Ganu.

TRUE — Philip's question and Das Ganu's demand for sakshatkara got the same answer.
8

Baba kept the dhuni lit because He performed the Vedic fire ritual (agnihotra).

FALSE — Baba never did Vedic fire ritual; the dhuni burns "to burn our sins".
