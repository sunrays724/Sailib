SaiLib Listen. Made for ears, not eyes.

This page holds the same teachings as the main SaiLib site. It is built for a person who cannot see the screen. There are no pictures here. Nothing moves on its own. Nothing opens without you asking. Everything can be heard, and everything works with a keyboard alone.

How this page works

  1. Six sections, in order. Reflection, latest satsang, digests, books, songs, and today in history. Nothing else competes for attention.
  2. Listen buttons. Every section has a “Listen to this section” button. It reads that section aloud in your device’s voice. Nothing plays by itself, so it never talks over your screen reader.
  3. Keyboard. Press keys 1 to 6 to jump to a section. Press slash (/) to jump to search. Tab moves forward, one thing at a time, in reading order.
  4. Links say where they go. Every link names its destination and its length, for example “Watch on YouTube, 2 hours 8 minutes, opens YouTube.” There is no “click here.”
  5. Your settings stay. Text size, contrast, and reading speed are remembered on your device.

Saturday satsang with Sri Babuji, 2 hours 8 minutes. In his own words, on handing the reins of life to the Guru, sitting still as the highest yoga, helplessness rather than ignorance as our basic problem, and nishta and saburi as the two coins of dakshina.

About this page

SaiLib Listen lives at pathofsai.com/accessibility. Its short, easy-to-say address is pathofsai.com/listen. Both open this same page.

It is the listening version of SaiLib, the library of Sri Babuji’s teachings at pathofsai.com. The main site has pictures, themes, and visual search. This page has the same teachings, in words and sound.

No pictures are used on this page, so nothing here depends on seeing. No sound plays by itself. No information is asked from you. Links to YouTube and to PDF files open those services, which have their own accessibility.
